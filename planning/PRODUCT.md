# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Urban office workers, hostel residents, corporate buyers, event hosts, and the internal admin team. Customers need fast meal subscriptions, custom food requests, frozen goods ordering, event catering inquiries, and manual payment proof submission. Admins need a controlled workspace for validating bank transfers and confirming paid orders.

## Product Purpose
URBAN TIFFIN AND EVENTS sells premium homemade meal subscriptions, frozen goods, and bespoke event catering/decor services. The product must convert visitors into paid customers, collect structured order information, support manual bank-transfer verification, and give admins a reliable pending-payment control desk.

## Brand Personality
Grounded, tactile, and premium. The interface should feel like a high-end printed hospitality catalog adapted into a disciplined operations app: confident, calm, and precise.

## Anti-references
No generic SaaS landing page, no glassmorphism, no backdrop blur, no fuzzy gradients, no neon glow, no sterile pure-white surfaces, no over-rounded cards, no decorative template icon grids, and no fake business placeholders.

## Design Principles
- Brand tokens are infrastructure: every surface uses the logo-derived green, ochre, cream, and dark cream.
- Luxury comes from spacing, alignment, type contrast, and sharp rules, not visual effects.
- Ordering stays direct: users should always know what they are buying, what payment proof is needed, and what happens next.
- Admin workflows are dense and trustworthy: pending money, customer identity, order context, and receipt imagery are visible without visual noise.
- Motion explains state changes only: tier switches, drawers, row expansion, upload feedback, and review actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable forms and drawers, visible focus states, semantic buttons and labels, useful alt text, and reduced-motion fallbacks for Framer Motion interactions.
